Setting up the two phones
  1. Open this page on both phones over HTTPS. Choose Leader on one and Follower on the other, with the same slot length.
  2. Lay the leader on a table, back up. Put a fingertip over its camera and flashlight.
  3. Press the follower's back down on top of the finger, so the finger sits between the two lenses.
  4. Press Start on the follower first, then on the leader. Keep still and dim the room.
  5. The leader lights for one slot, then goes dark. The follower sees that, waits the gap, then lights for one slot. Repeat.

Flashlight control works in Chrome on Android. An iPhone can only record; as follower it captures the leader's light through the finger but cannot light back. The ratio figure is a raw red-to-green comparison, not blood oxygen. This is a research prototype and gives no medical readings.
